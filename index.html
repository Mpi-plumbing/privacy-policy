MPI Plumbing App – Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026 · MPI Plumbing Corp

This policy explains what the MPI Plumbing mobile app for Android (package com.mpiplumbing.corp) collects and how MPI Plumbing Corp uses it. The app is an internal work tool for MPI Plumbing Corp employees to order job materials, track orders and view project budgets. Accounts are issued by MPI Plumbing Corp; there is no public sign-up.

Information we collect

  • Sign-in details. Your work email address and password are sent to MPI Plumbing Corp's server to sign you in. The password is not stored on your device; the app keeps a sign-in token until you sign out.
  • Account details. Your name, email address, phone number and account ID. You can update your name and phone number in the app.
  • Orders you create. Materials and quantities, items added by hand, the job and job-site address, date and time, assigned manager, PO number and notes.
  • Push notification token. A device token from Firebase Cloud Messaging, linked to your account, so the app can deliver notifications about your orders.
  • App usage information. The app includes Google Analytics for Firebase, which automatically collects usage and device information: an app instance ID, the device's advertising ID, device model, operating system, language, app version, approximate region (derived from the IP address) and events such as app opens, sessions and screens viewed.

The app does not access your camera, photos, contacts, microphone or precise device location. It contains no advertising and no crash-reporting service.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and operate the app: placing and tracking material orders, showing project budgets and sending order updates.
  • To deliver push notifications to your device.
  • To understand how the app is used and to improve it.

MPI Plumbing Corp does not sell your personal information and does not use it for advertising.

Who receives it

Your information is stored on MPI Plumbing Corp's own servers and processed by the service providers that run them for us, including our hosting provider and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging and Google Analytics for Firebase, under Google's Privacy Policy). Authorized MPI Plumbing Corp administrators can access account, order and analytics information as needed to run the business. We do not share your information with anyone else except where the law requires it.

Security

All data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Order drafts, the material catalog and your sign-in token are kept in the app's private storage on your device. Signing out clears your saved sign-in session.

Retention

Account and order records are kept by MPI Plumbing Corp for as long as needed to run the business and to meet legal and accounting obligations. Analytics data is retained according to our Firebase project settings. Contact us at the email below for details.

Deleting your account and data

The app has no in-app sign-up or account deletion. To close your account or delete your personal data, email nlandau@mpiplumbingcorp.com with the subject "Account deletion request" from the email address on your account. Tell us whether you want the account closed, your personal data deleted, or both. MPI Plumbing Corp will verify the request, carry it out, and let you know of any business records that must be kept for legal or accounting reasons.

Children

The app is for MPI Plumbing Corp employees only and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: MPI Plumbing Corp, nlandau@mpiplumbingcorp.com, mpiplumbingcorp.com.
